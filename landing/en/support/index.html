A valid support email has not been provided. Add a working contact address before App Store submission.

Try these first

  • Quit and reopen Pinly.
  • Check location, camera, or notification permission under iOS Settings → Pinly.
  • If route calculation fails, verify that each stop has a valid address/location.
  • If Pro is missing, use Profile → Pinly Pro → Restore Purchases.

Diagnostics

Profile → Diagnostics shows recent on-device crash/hang summaries. A useful report includes app version, iOS version, reproduction steps, and an optional screenshot. Share precise location, private notes, or photos only when necessary and intentionally.

Subscription management

Apple manages cancellation and renewal. Open Apple subscriptions or use Restore Purchases in Pinly.

Privacy and deletion

V1 has no server account. Delete local places/routes in the app or uninstall Pinly to remove its local container. See Privacy Choices for ads and permissions.
