FAQ

Short answers

Is Pinly free?

Yes. Saving places, route planning, and navigation are free. Pro adds GPX/PDF export and removes ads.

Do I need an account?

No. V1 is personal and local-first; Sign in with Apple and community features are disabled.

Where is my data?

Places, notes, profile details, and photos are stored in the app’s space on your device. Third-party analytics, crash reporting, ads, and subscription services may process limited technical data as described in the privacy policy.

Why is there no Pro price here?

Prices may vary by country and App Store account. The current amount is shown by the App Store inside the app.
