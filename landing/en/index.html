FAQ

Short answers

Is Pinly free?

Yes. Saving places, route planning, and navigation are free. Pro adds GPX/PDF export and removes ads.

Do I need an account?

No. V1 is personal and local-first; Sign in with Apple and community features are disabled.

Where is my data?

Places, notes, profile details, and photos are stored in the app’s space on your device. Analytics, crash, advertising, and subscription providers may process limited technical data described in the privacy policy.

Why is there no Pro price here?

Prices vary by country and App Store account. The current amount appears in the App Store purchase screen inside the app.
