Privacy Choices

Change permissions, revisit ad consent when available, and remove local data.

Ads and tracking

  1. Open Profile → Privacy Choices in Pinly.
  2. Use “Ad Privacy Options” to reopen Google’s UMP form when required for your region.
  3. For Apple tracking permission, open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and change Pinly’s setting.

Refusing tracking does not disable core app features. AdMob requests do not start without the applicable consent state. Pro users do not see ads.

Location, Health, camera, and notifications

Use iOS Settings → Pinly to change location, camera, photo, and notification access. Manage Health access in Health → profile → Apps and Services → Pinly. Pinly does not write Health data.

Delete local data

Delete individual places and routes in their lists. Uninstalling Pinly removes its on-device app container. V1 has no Pinly server account to delete because social account creation is disabled.

Subscription

Deleting the app or local data does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage it in Apple subscription settings.

A privacy-request email must be added before production release.
