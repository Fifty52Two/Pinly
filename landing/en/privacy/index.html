Privacy Policy

1. Scope and controller

This policy covers the Pinly iOS app and marketing website. In V1, app accounts, Sign in with Apple, public profiles, community feeds, and social route publishing are disabled. The controller’s full identity and contact details must be added before release.

A Swarm/Foursquare archive is selected by the user and converted into places in the app. Because V1 social services are off, this content is not sent to Supabase community tables.

3. Purposes and permissions

  • Provide place, route, navigation, sharing, and route-memory features.
  • Use location—with permission—to plan routes, find nearby places, and follow a walk.
  • Use HealthKit—with permission—to show route statistics.
  • Schedule reminders only after the user acts on the relevant permission prompt.
  • Understand app use, diagnose failures, and improve reliability.
  • Show ads to Free users according to consent/platform permission and keep Pro ad-free.
  • Verify purchases, restores, and Pro access.

Location, camera/photos, Health, notification, and tracking permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. Refusing a permission does not block all core use, but the related feature may be limited.

4. Service providers

  • Apple: App Store/StoreKit, MapKit, HealthKit, notifications, and OS services. Apple Privacy.
  • Google: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, and User Messaging Platform. Firebase privacy and security; Google advertising technologies.
  • RevenueCat: subscription and entitlement verification. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Provider production settings and retention controls must be verified before release.

5. Retention, deletion, and security

Local content may remain until the user deletes it or removes the app. Related photo files may be removed when records are deleted; uninstalling removes the app’s local container. Provider retention follows their policies and configured settings.

Pinly relies on HTTPS in transit, the iOS app sandbox, and provider security controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Choices and rights

Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. Where applicable, Profile → Privacy Choices can reopen the ad privacy options form. See Privacy Choices.

A valid privacy contact must be added for access, correction, deletion, objection, and consent-withdrawal requests. V1 has no Pinly server account; local data can be removed in the app or by uninstalling it. Removing local data does not cancel an App Store subscription.

7. Children and changes

Pinly is not designed as a child-directed service. The optional profile may be skipped. The minimum-age and market strategy requires legal review before release.

This policy will be updated when data practices change, with appropriate notice for material changes.
